Iris for YouTube lets you send YouTube videos to your friends without leaving YouTube.

**Send**

- Click the Send button under any video, or right-click a video link, and pick your friends.
- Add a short note (up to 140 characters) and an optional start time.

**Receive**

- Iris adds its own entry to YouTube's sidebar. A dot appears there when something new arrives.
- The Iris page has three tabs: Inbox, Sent and Friends.
- Recent videos from friends also show up in a "From friends" row at the top of YouTube's home page.

**Private by design**

- No read receipts. Your friends never find out whether you watched what they sent.
- People can only find you by typing your exact handle.
- Delete your account at any time from the Iris menu. Everything is removed immediately.

**Getting started**

Sign in with Google, pick a handle, and send a friend request. Iris needs nothing beyond your Google name, email and profile picture.
